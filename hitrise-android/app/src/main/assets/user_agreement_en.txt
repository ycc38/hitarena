HitArena User Agreement

1. Scope
This agreement applies to the HitArena app and services provided by Shaoxing Weimai Technology Co., Ltd., including five-target Bluetooth connections, match timing and scoring, live competition, competition results and rankings, HitArena Certified Device status, and trusted online rankings. Please also read the HitArena Privacy Policy.

2. Services
HitArena can connect up to five Bluetooth sensors that satisfy the existing device-name filter, record punch counts and relative force for multiplayer matches, calculate results under Punch Count, Power, or Combined rules, and save results locally. Network features depend on permissions, connectivity, cloud availability, and the manufacturer certification registry.

3. Optional Match Data Upload
Match Data Upload is off by default. If you do not enable it, match records stay on the device and do not enter the cloud match database or trusted rankings.

Selecting “Agree & Enable” authorizes HitArena to process and upload eligible match data within the Privacy Policy. You can turn the option off at any time. This stops future and unfinished uploads but does not automatically delete records already uploaded.

Consent alone does not make every match eligible. The server accepts a match as trusted only if every sensor used is in the manufacturer-maintained registry, device assignments and underlying data are complete, and server-side score and security checks pass. A match involving an uncertified or unverified sensor, incomplete connection or data, or failed validation remains local only. Manufacturer certification data can be viewed but cannot be added or edited by app users.

4. Uploaded and Displayed Information
An eligible upload may include match ID and time, rules and settings, player nicknames, Bluetooth names, hashed device addresses, punch-by-punch relative force and timing, round statistics, scores, ranks, and awards. Trusted rankings may display nickname, category, score, rank, time, and certification status.

Only enter player names or nicknames you are entitled to use. A multiplayer match organizer must inform participants about this agreement and the Privacy Policy and obtain appropriate permission before enabling upload. Participants who do not agree should use local-only mode.

5. Scoring and Device Limitations
The app applies the rules shown for Punch Count, Power, and Combined matches. Relative force is an uncalibrated sensor score without kilogram, kilogram-force, or newton units. Installation, sensor variance, Bluetooth conditions, punch technique, and environment can affect results. Certification confirms a registered device type and data validation; it does not guarantee error-free data or constitute an official sports ruling.
